The flaws are hiding in the foundations that the world runs on.

Vektorium Inc.25 April 2026

Originally published on LinkedIn. The publication text below is retained as published.

View original publication (opens in a new tab) ↗
The flaws are hiding in the foundations that the world runs on. Vulnerabilities that decades of conventional cybersecurity and a $250 billion industry could not, and had never, found. Cypher Sentinel predicted this moment. It was built in anticipation of what Glasswing reveals. The Office of the Director of National Intelligence released the 2026 Annual Threat Assessment of the U.S. Intelligence Community. For the first time, AI threats are treated as a structural force reshaping competition across all actors and all domains simultaneously. China. Russia. Iran. North Korea. Each weaponizing AI to accelerate cyber operations, target critical infrastructure, and erode strategic advantage, not in the future, but now. That is where, alongside Glasswing, the picture becomes complete. The offensive side: our adversaries are using the new toolbox to attack faster, deeper, and more autonomously than any previous generation of threats. It is two sides of the same coin. And in the space between them stands every nation, every critical infrastructure operator, every enterprise - facing the question we posed two weeks ago: Will you own your defense, or rent it? The question is no longer rhetorical. It is an operational one. If AI is the defining technology of strategic competition, and the intelligence community of the most powerful nation on earth now says it is, then sovereign defense is not a feature. It is an existential requirement. A platform that does not merely detect threats but anticipates them. That does not wait for a consortium to disclose vulnerabilities but finds them within its own perimeter, under its own control. That operates autonomously; both as a vector and as a membrane, both as a spear and as a shield, within sovereign infrastructure with zero external dependencies. Cypher Sentinel was engineered for this exact convergence of realities. Not for a world where AI might become a threat. For a world where it already is one. It anticipated this reality and helped shape these new defenses. The current conventional model - fragmented, reactive, dependent - has been declared structurally inadequate. It has now been declared a national security risk by the intelligence community itself. The era of sovereign autonomous defense is not approaching. It has arrived. We built it.
