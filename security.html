Security & Trust

Trust should be inspectable.

Our public website, demonstration environments, and Cypher Sentinel product are distinct systems. This page explains the website's boundaries, how to report a concern, and what to establish during a product evaluation.

Website information reviewed 25 October 2026.

A deliberately small public website

Static delivery

The public site serves static pages and assets through Vercel. It does not provide customer sign-in, file uploads, payment collection, or access to Cypher Sentinel operational data.

Browser protections

HTTPS and restrictive browser policies limit permitted script sources, framing, and sensitive browser features. These controls reduce exposure; they are not a guarantee that a website cannot be compromised.

Optional measurement

Optional Google Analytics loads only after an affirmative choice on the public production website. Essential-only browsing remains available. You can revisit the choice using Privacy settings.

Explicit demo boundaries

External demos load only when requested and run in restricted frames. They illustrate workflows using demonstration data; they do not prove production performance or customer deployment.

Report a security concern

Email security@vektorium.io with a concise description, the affected public URL, and safe reproduction steps. If delivery fails, use the contact page. Our machine-readable disclosure contact is available in security.txt.

  • Share only the minimum evidence needed to explain the issue. Do not send passwords, access tokens, personal data, customer records, or confidential operational information.
  • Do not access other people's data, disrupt availability, attempt persistence, or run high-volume automated tests.
  • This page is not permission to test third-party infrastructure or the separate demo applications. Agree an appropriate scope with us before intrusive testing.

No bug-bounty payment or response-time guarantee is offered by this page.

Product assurance is a separate evaluation

Cypher Sentinel is Vektorium's cybersecurity platform. This page describes the corporate website's controls, not an assessment of the platform's current implementation. Product assurance should be reviewed against the relevant version, configuration, and supporting evidence.

For a scoped evaluation, establish the release and supported capabilities, deployment and data boundaries, integration requirements, test evidence, known limitations, and operational responsibilities. Sensitive technical detail can be discussed under an appropriate confidentiality agreement.

Use of a named standard or cryptographic algorithm does not, by itself, establish independent certification, regulatory compliance, or validated implementation. Any assurance claim must identify its scope and supporting evidence.

Keep confidential information off public channels

Do not enter confidential material into public demos or send it in an initial email. Agree a suitable exchange method first. For website data practices, see the Privacy Policy; for website terms, see the Terms of Use.
