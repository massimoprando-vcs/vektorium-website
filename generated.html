About this demonstration and its boundaries

The desktop and mobile simulations are separate applications hosted at cypher-sentinel-demo.vercel.app and mobile-gold-five.vercel.app. Their behavior and availability are separate from this static website.

The embedded frame is limited to script execution, its own origin, and fullscreen. This page does not grant it camera, microphone, location, motion-sensor, clipboard, popup, or top-level navigation permissions. These restrictions are a precaution, not a security certification of the demonstration.

For product decisions, agree a bounded evaluation and review the relevant evidence with Vektorium. A visualization alone cannot establish deployment suitability, operational performance, or compliance.
